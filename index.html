    Graduation project

    A year on one problem

    Privacy-preserving MQTT messaging

    Sabancı University · Sep 2022 – Jun 2023

    MQTT is the messaging protocol half the IoT world runs on, and it leaks more than people assume. Working in a pair, we picked apart the protocol's weaknesses and designed three schemes to close them — covering confidentiality, authentication, authorisation, integrity and user privacy.

    We then implemented all three across the server and client modules: ephemeral Diffie–Hellman for key exchange, authenticated encryption with AES, HMAC signatures and salting.
